A little less ding. A little more dog.

Choose a cut, click to place the playhead, or drag to select a range.

One source view per sound. A/B/C selects a range; Full clip selects the whole recording. Clicking keeps the selection and plays from that position to the next marked end, or the recording’s end. Spectrograms show 20 Hz–12 kHz on a log scale; brighter means stronger. Waveform heights are scaled individually. Arrow keys move a selection by 50 ms.

Playback uses the source recordings, with explicitly labeled pitch alternatives where available. Pitch alternatives preserve duration; “Original” keeps the unedited source. Earlier cut files are retained locally. Dog recordings are CC0; Herdr cues retain Apache-2.0. Sources and edit history.
